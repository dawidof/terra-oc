# TerraAuto content and brand notes

## 1. Brand source

Primary social reference supplied by project owner:
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/terraauto_/

At planning time Instagram could not be fetched directly by the browsing environment.

Public recent TerraAuto vehicle listings were available through indexed classifieds and confirm recurring brand/business information.

## 2. Verified recurring public positioning

Current public TerraAuto listings repeatedly state:

- direct import from China;
- purchase directly from manufacturers / without intermediaries;
- vehicle registration can be made to the client or the client's company;
- individual color and configuration;
- typical delivery period shown as 20–30 days;
- payment under an official contract / bank transfer and other payment methods;
- more than three years of China vehicle-import experience;
- Tashkent / Sergeli district;
- Aysel customs warehouse;
- Index auto market;
- Instagram `TerraAuto_`;
- YouTube `TerraAutoUz`.

Phone numbers and exact address should be verified directly with TerraAuto before publication.

## 3. Website tone

Russian copy should be:

- clear;
- modern;
- confident;
- factual;
- not overloaded with slogans;
- transparent about estimates.

Avoid claims such as:
- "самые дешёвые";
- "100% лучшая цена";
- "гарантированно дешевле";
unless the owner can substantiate them.

## 5. Important commercial concepts

Use consistently:

### Source price
Clearly label:
- цена авто;
- auction price;
- CIP;
- other basis.

### Estimated turnkey price
Use:
`Ориентировочная стоимость под ключ`

Never mix source price with final price without labeling.

### Delivery
If TerraAuto confirms 20–30 days:
`Ориентировочный срок доставки: 20–30 дней`

Avoid promise language unless contractually guaranteed.

## 6. Social proof

Reviews should ideally include:
- actual delivered vehicle;
- customer photo or vehicle photo;
- real short quote;
- source link if public and permitted.

Do not fabricate reviews for demo without marking them as placeholders.

## 7. Visual direction

Use NCars as inspiration for:
- clean layout;
- large automotive imagery;
- modern cards;
- strong hero;
- clear CTAs;
- calculator entry point.

Do not clone its visual identity.

Use Gonzo as reference for:
- depth of vehicle data;
- trim differences;
- technical specification grouping;
- Uzbekistan-focused import terminology.

Do not copy non-vehicle site copy.

## 8. Content migration policy

Competitor-derived car data should be treated as initial research/input.

Before public production:
- technical facts: verify where feasible;
- descriptions: rewrite;
- photos: ensure usage rights;
- prices: update;
- customs estimates: verify;
- model years/trims: validate.
